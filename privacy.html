Privacy Policy - VideoPoster

Last updated: October 20, 2026

1. Scope

This privacy policy describes how VideoPoster — a personal video-publishing utility — handles information. VideoPoster connects to TikTok accounts through TikTok's official Content Posting API and "Login with TikTok" OAuth flow.

2. What we access

When an account holder authorizes VideoPoster, the app receives a limited OAuth access token from TikTok that allows: - publishing videos to the authorized account (Content Posting API, scope video.publish / video.upload); - basic account identifier information (open_id) returned by the OAuth flow. VideoPoster does NOT request and does not receive your TikTok password, follower lists, private messages, analytics data, app lists, or any data beyond the publishing scopes listed above.

3. How data is stored

VideoPoster runs on a single private computer owned by the operator. Tokens are stored in a local configuration file on this computer only. VideoPoster does not use external servers, cloud databases, third-party analytics, cookies, or advertising networks to process your data. No personal data is transmitted to anyone other than TikTok's own API endpoints.

4. What we upload

When publishing, VideoPoster transmits the video file and the caption text you provide directly to TikTok's upload endpoints. This content is handled by TikTok under TikTok's own privacy policy.

5. Data retention

Tokens are kept only as long as needed to publish content for the account holder, and are deleted on request. If you revoke the app in your TikTok settings, the corresponding token is invalidated by TikTok and removed from local storage by the operator upon request.

6. Your rights

Since VideoPoster stores essentially no personal data beyond access tokens, no profile, analytics or behavioral data is collected. You may request deletion of your token and any associated records at any time by contacting the owner, or simply remove the app's authorization in your TikTok account settings.

7. Contact

Contact the owner through the contact channels provided during the authorization flow.
