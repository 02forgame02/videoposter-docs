Personal tool

VideoPoster

VideoPoster is a small personal utility used by its owner to publish videos to their own TikTok accounts through TikTok's official Content Posting API ("Login with TikTok" OAuth).

Key facts

  • Only the owner's own authorized accounts are used — no third-party users.
  • We do not collect or store personal data on external servers; only OAuth tokens are kept locally.
  • Access may be revoked at any time in your TikTok settings.
